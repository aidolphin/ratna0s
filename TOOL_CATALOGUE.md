## Naming and verification

- Names are transliterations where no settled trade term exists; a native term is not
  invented for a tool that traders call by its English name.
- Acid test kits and diamond testers are screening aids, not certification. Results must
  not be printed as a certificate or legal assay.
- Calibration status, serial numbers and service dates are not tracked yet.
